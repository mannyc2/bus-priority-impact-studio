## Review standard and evidence

Approval required an authoritative, exact route or route-family assertion for a
realized operational onset, including exact local-versus-SBS identity; a clean
treatment phase; no unresolved date, identity, duplicate, phase, or nearby
treatment ambiguity; and an observed study window inside 2023-04 through
2026-03 with the implementation month excluded and at least four usable months
on both sides.

The official MTA dataset defines each record at bus-route and program
granularity and defines Implementation Date as the date the program took effect:
https://data.ny.gov/Transportation/MTA-Bus-Automated-Camera-Enforced-Routes-Beginning/ki2b-sg5y

The official NYC DOT dataset is segment-granular:
https://data.cityofnewyork.us/Transportation/Bus-Lanes-Local-Streets/ycrg-ses3
The local event builder associates those lane pieces to routes by stop
proximity or street-name matching and promotes the latest matched opening
month. That establishes nearby infrastructure, not an authoritative
route-specific operational onset. Exact SBS fields were treated as evidence
for the SBS identity only, never for the corresponding local route.

Current segment-spine eligibility was also checked. Plan 074 admits
series_ready and series_ready_with_gaps routes; needs_pattern_review was
conservatively rejected. Same-route interventions inside the outcome window
were treated as unresolved overlap.
