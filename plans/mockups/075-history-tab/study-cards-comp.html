Decisions — round 2

Answer by number. Approvals become the plan-075 acceptance target; rejections become doctrine rules.
Resolved in round 1: Variant B layout wins (D1). Round 2: checks never render on the card face (provenance-only); one consolidated header metric; window-band shading dropped; chart cards align to the shadcn chart-card anatomy. Round 3: verbosity pass — “vs controls” stat label, “No clear change” null copy, header window line and footer range line deleted, legends shortened.
D10
Single metric placement: header-right stat block, as shown?
Mirrors the shadcn interactive-chart header and keeps the number adjacent to the chart it summarizes. Alternative: left-aligned above the chart under the title. Default: header-right as shown.
D11
Chart series: two overlaid areas (treated vs matched controls), or a single “gap vs controls” series?
Two series read as “my bus vs similar streets” — immediately legible. A single difference series is statistically tighter but abstract. Default: two series as shown.
D12
Hover tooltip (shown open on B1): approve the interaction?
It is the stock shadcn ChartTooltipContent restyled to app tokens — free with the primitives we already use. Default: yes.
D13
The null card’s tolling caveat: keep the one-sentence footer note + chart marker (B2), or move it fully into provenance?
It is the last visible trace of the former checks — kept because it qualifies a public claim on a Manhattan route in plain words. Default: keep as shown.
D14
Footer trend icon (13px arrow / flat line): keep or drop?
Part of the shadcn footer anatomy; tone-colored, never a verdict word. Default: keep; drop if it reads decorative.
D15
Carried from round 1, defaults stand unless vetoed: CI copy (D7), deep-link ring (D8), section sub-line (D9).
Long-form “95% CI +0.06 to +0.39” on cards, compact “+0.22 (+0.06 to +0.39) mph” on rows; 2px MTA-blue fading ring; “4 evaluations, 3 with matched-segment studies.” Default: all three as shown.
D16
Null display copy “No clear change” — approved?
Shorter than “No detectable change” while keeping the hedge; the artifact enum stays no_detectable_change, so this is copy only. Default: as shown.
D17
The legacy no-study card (B4) still shows today’s long window line. Extend the deletion to it?
Plan 075 pins no-study cards byte-identical to today; trimming their window line is a one-line scope addition in the same file. Default: leave unchanged in 075; note it for a later sweep.
