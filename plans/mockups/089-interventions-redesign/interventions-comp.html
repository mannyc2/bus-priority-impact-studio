Decisions — round 4

Answer by number. Approvals become the plan's acceptance target; rejections ratchet into the doctrine lint.
Resolved: Variant A (r1); hero band + table dress (r2); two surfaces, stat-tab header, toolbar selects, in-card histogram (r3: D17, D18, D20). Rejected in round 3 review: the hero forest plot, the “Studied” tab, and the labeled year-divider rows (D15 reversed) — all replaced below.
D22
Text-only hero with the mono stat line — approve?
No chart in the hero; the stat line is the page's one self-summary. Default: yes.
D23
Documented / Planned as the only tabs; “studied only” as a toolbar checkbox?
Studied is an attribute, not a status — it will spread across both partitions as coverage grows. Default: yes.
D24
Margin years with counts + firmer year hairline, replacing divider rows (A4)?
The grouping structure moves into the ledger margin where a ledger keeps it. Planned tab reuses it with plan names. Default: yes.
D25
Kind badge removed from rows?
It duplicated the title on nearly every row; kind remains filterable in the toolbar and stated by the title copy. Status badges (proposed/scheduled) stay in the Planned tab, where they carry real information. Default: yes.
D26
Right-column compression: number, CI, register-as-link?
“matched-segment study →” / “descriptive study →” are the links; peer-adjusted rows stay unlinked and muted. Four lines become at most three, usually two. Default: yes.
D27
Hero copy (carried since round 2): keep “What the city built for buses — and what it changed.”?
Alternatives: “Every bus priority intervention, on the record.” / “The street changes New York made for its buses.” Default: keep.
